🍲 Coming soon to Android and PC

You washed up on an island. The island is the inside of a bowl. The bowl is very, very old, and it's about to be switched back on.

BowlBound is a goofy open-world survival game with real combat, base building, and a story to uncover. By day, you chop, mine, and craft. By night, the Gloam crawl out of the Stir, and you hold the line. Every fifth night is a Horde Night, and each one is harder than the last.

👉 Follow this page to get notified at launch.

🪓 Survive

  • Gather and craft: chop, mine, fish, and cook your way from a wooden club to Ladlesteel gear.
  • Explore 12 hand-built regions: from the Shipwreck Coast to the Bowl's Edge, plus dungeons, ruins, and a kitchen deep under the lake.
  • Fill your Rewards inbox: earn caches from settlement quests, achievements, defeated Guardians, and Horde Nights.

🧱 Build

  • Build your way: place blocks one at a time, or use the Builder's Hammer to raise whole houses, towers, and walls.
  • Fortify your base: palisades, iron bulwarks, chomper traps, ember grates, gravy puddles, and an alarm bell.
  • Wire up a power grid: burner cells, sun skillets, jelly batteries, and copper pylons run your Zap Coils, Fryer Turrets, Gravy Cannons, and Floodlights.

⚔️ Fight

  • Master Souls-style combat: lock on, read the wind-up, roll through it, then parry and riposte.
  • Learn every enemy: each one has its own moveset, and the big ones have poise you have to break.
  • Face the bosses: four Flavor-Wardens guard the four Heartgems, and a final boss waits under the lake.

🐔 Make friends (or enemies)

  • Meet four settlements: municipal chickens, golf-cart-driving deer, miner goats, and a town of living furniture, each with its own quests, shops, and standing requests.
  • Mind your Settlement Karma: help a settlement, and its people will love you. Hurt them, and they will send war parties.

📱 Made for phones, great on PC

  • Play with anything: full touch controls, a controller, or keyboard and mouse.
  • Switch views anytime: play in first or third person, and swap whenever you like.
  • Bring an older phone: auto performance mode keeps things smooth.
  • Play offline: no account, no internet connection, and no pay-to-win.

Just you, a bowl, and a lot of soup-related danger.
