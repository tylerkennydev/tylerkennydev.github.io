Privacy Policy – Poopy Zombies

By Tyler Kenny Games · Last updated: October 27, 2026 · Applies to Poopy Zombies v4.5.6 on Google Play

Poopy Zombies is made by one indie dev. This page explains what data the game uses, who handles it, and how to get it deleted. Poopy Zombies has no ads.

What we collect, and why

  • Online multiplayer (5v5, Infected, Rival and Zombies Online): online play runs on Epic Online Services (EOS). When you go online, Epic creates an anonymous player ID tied to your device. It also gets your device model so it can set that ID up. No Epic account is needed. To run lobbies, matchmaking and peer-to-peer connections, Epic processes your IP address, and so do the other players' devices you connect to. See https://www.epicgames.com/site/privacy-policy
  • Display name and clan tag: other players see your in-game name and clan tag in lobbies, in matches and on leaderboards. They're public, so don't use your real name.
  • Stats, leaderboards and achievements: Epic stores your player ID, name, scores, best rounds and stats for online leaderboards and achievements.
  • Google Play Games Services (Android): if you're signed in to Play Games, Google handles your Play Games profile (gamertag), achievements and leaderboard scores. It also stores an optional cloud save in Saved Games. You can unlink it in Settings > Account. See https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Cloud save (optional, on by default): when you're online, a copy of your save is backed up with Epic under your anonymous player ID, and with Google Play Games if you're linked. The copy includes progress, settings and purchase records. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • In-app purchases: Google Play Billing handles Doo-bloon purchases. We never see your card or payment details. The game keeps a purchase token with your save so that it credits each purchase only once.
  • Voice chat (optional, off by default): if you turn it on, your voice goes live through Epic to the other players in your lobby. We don't record it.
  • Player reports: if you report a player, the report goes to Epic and to us for review. It includes both player IDs, the reason, your optional note and match info (mode, map, match ID and game version).
  • Time check: to stop clock cheats on daily and timed content, the game asks Cloudflare or Google for the current time. Like any web request, this sends your IP address to that server. No game data is sent.
  • Crashes and performance: we don't add any analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Google Play may share crash and performance reports (Android vitals) with us, based on your device settings.
  • Email: if you email us, for example with the in-game Feedback or Report a Bug button, we get your email address and whatever you send. The button fills in your game version and device model.

What stays on your phone

  • Your progress, skins, Doo-bloons, settings and Easter egg progress are saved on your device.
  • Your blocked-player list and recent players stay on your device.
  • Exported save files and save codes stay wherever you put them. We never see them.
  • Local Wi-Fi play stays on your local network.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no ad networks and no advertising ID.
  • We don't collect your real name, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We never sell your data. We only share it with Epic (online play) and Google (Play Games, purchases and app diagnostics) so the game can run.

Your choices

  • You can play offline, solo or on local Wi-Fi without any online features. You can also switch online off in the game.
  • Voice chat and cloud save can be turned on or off in Settings.
  • Under Online, Delete My Online Data deletes your Epic cloud save and online player ID and turns online off.
  • To delete leaderboard entries, reports or anything else we hold, email us and we'll remove it.
  • Data sent over the internet is encrypted in transit.

To delete your account and data, see delete your Poopy Zombies account & data.

Kids

Poopy Zombies is rated Everyone 10+, but it isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you think a child under 13 has shared data with us, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above.
