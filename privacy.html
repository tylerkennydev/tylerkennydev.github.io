Privacy Policy – Poopy Zombies

By Tyler Kenny Games · Updated October 9, 2026

What we collect, and why

  • Ads: Google AdMob may collect your advertising ID, device info, IP address and approximate location to show and measure ads. See https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Online multiplayer: we use Epic Online Services for online play. When you play online, Epic gives your device an anonymous player ID linked to your device, and processes your IP address to connect you to other players. No Epic account is needed. See https://www.epicgames.com/site/privacy-policy
  • Username and clan tag: shown to other players in online matches, on leaderboards and in reports. They're public, so don't use your real name.
  • Leaderboards and stats: your username, player ID, scores and best rounds are stored by Supabase and Epic to run leaderboards.
  • Voice chat (optional, off by default): if you turn it on, your voice goes live to the other players in your lobby through Epic. We don't record it.
  • Player reports: if you report a player, the report, both player IDs and the match info go to Epic and to us for review.
  • Cloud save backup (optional): if you turn it on, your save data is stored with Epic under your anonymous player ID.

What stays on your phone

  • Your progress, skins, Doo-bloons, settings and Easter egg progress are saved on your device.
  • Exported save files and save codes stay wherever you put them. We never see them.
  • Local Wi-Fi play stays on your local network.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect your real name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We never sell your data. We only share it with Google (ads), Epic (online play) and Supabase (leaderboards), so the game can run.

Your choices

  • You can turn off personalized ads in your phone's settings.
  • You can play offline, solo or on local Wi-Fi without using online features.
  • To delete your leaderboard entry, online player data or cloud save, email us and we'll remove it.
  • Data sent over the internet is encrypted in transit.

Kids

Poopy Zombies isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above.
