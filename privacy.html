Privacy Policy – Poopy Zombies

By Tyler Kenny Games · Updated 9-28-26

Ads: Google AdMob may collect your advertising ID, device info and IP address to show ads (policies.google.com/privacy).

Leaderboard (optional): stores your chosen nickname, a random player ID and your scores. It's public, so don't use your real name.

Wi-Fi play stays on your local network. Your progress stays on your device.

We don't collect names, emails, location or photos, and we never sell your data. We only share with Google (ads) and Supabase (leaderboard).

Not meant for kids under 13. You can turn off personalized ads in your phone settings. To delete your leaderboard entry, email us.
