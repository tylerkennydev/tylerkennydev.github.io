Poopy Zombies Is Coming to Google Play Soon! + Achievements & Daily Rewards 🧟🧻

Tyler Kenny · Sep 29, 2026

Big news: Poopy Zombies is almost ready for its full Google Play launch! The alpha test is underway with a great group of testers, and the public release is coming soon.

Here's everything that's new since the last devlog.

🏅 27 ACHIEVEMENTS

From FIRST FLUSH to BOSS OF BOSSES, there are now 27 achievements to chase: kill milestones, surviving to round 30, beating all 4 bosses, finishing every easter egg, reviving teammates, collecting skins and TP Sheets, keeping a daily streak, and more.

Every achievement pays out Doo-bloons, and anything you've already earned unlocks automatically the first time you open the update. Check your progress under ACHIEVEMENTS on the main menu.

📅 DAILY REWARDS

  • Daily login reward: play every day to build a streak, from 25 Doo-bloons on day 1 up to 150 on day 7. Watch an optional ad to double it. Miss a day and the streak starts over.
  • Daily challenge: a brand new goal every day, like "Reach round 8 on Dookie Desert" or "Kill 120 zombies in one game". Finish it in any game that day (co-op counts!) for a big payout.

🎓 NEW PLAYER TUTORIAL

New players now get a quick, skippable tutorial on their first game: move, shoot, buy a gun off the wall, and open a door. It adapts to whatever you're playing with: touch, controller, or keyboard and mouse. Veterans can replay it anytime from HOW TO PLAY.

🐞 BUG REPORT & FEEDBACK BUTTONS

Found a bug or have an idea? Reporting it now takes seconds:

  • FEEDBACK on the main menu
  • The bug button in the pause menu, right next to music and sound

Both open an email to me with your game version and device already filled in. The pause menu one even adds the map and round you were on, so I can find and fix problems faster.

🛠️ POLISH

  • Ads load more reliably on Android, and the double Doo-bloons button shows up even if the ad loads a moment late
  • A fresh new game icon starring the real in-game models
  • Lots of behind-the-scenes fixes

🙏 THANK YOU, TESTERS

A huge thank you to everyone testing the alpha on Google Play. Your time and bug reports are what's getting this game ready for launch. Keep them coming!

The full Play Store release is right around the corner. I'll post here the moment it's live.

Thanks for playing, and thanks for reading this far. Now go flush something. 🧻💩🧟
