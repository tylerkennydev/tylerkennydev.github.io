Delete your Poopy Zombies account & data

App: Poopy Zombies · Developer: Tyler Kenny Games

How to request deletion

Email TylerKennyGames@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Poopy Zombies data". Include your in-game username and clan tag, and your player ID if it's shown in Settings.

Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your username and clan tag
  • Your leaderboard entries and stats (stored with Supabase and Epic Online Services)
  • Your online player ID data
  • Your cloud save backup
  • Reports linked to you

What's kept

Nothing, except records we must keep by law. Those are deleted within 30 days.

Data on your phone

Progress saved only on your phone can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling Poopy Zombies.

How long it takes

We process deletion requests within 30 days.
