Modes

Zombies, 5v5 and co-op

Play in first person, third person or top-down.

Zombies

  • Round-based survival: earn points, open doors, flip on the power, run traps
  • Buy perks and upgrade guns at the Pack-a-Turd
  • Maps from Town and Sewer Subway to the Porta Potty Fest and a giant gut, plus the Heaven & Throne Pack
  • Floods, sandstorms, acid tides, easter eggs and wonder weapons
  • Endless mode in The Throne Room

5v5 Multiplayer alpha

  • Flush Wars: The Plungers vs The Brushes
  • Team Deathmatch and Domination (hold 3 thrones)
  • Infected and Rival 1v1
  • Quick Play with a lobby map vote, playlists and join by code
  • Bots fill empty seats so matches start fast
  • Change class mid-match

Co-op

  • Zombies Online: co-op with up to 4 players (alpha)
  • Split screen on one device, player 2 on a controller
  • Local Wi-Fi co-op
  • Solo with up to 3 AI squadmates
  • Solo, split screen and local Wi-Fi zombies work offline
