Poopy Zombies: Multiplayer FPS

Version 4.5.6 · Rated Everyone 10+ (Fantasy Violence, Mild Blood, Crude Humor) · Android · Early access

Poopy Zombies: Multiplayer FPS is a toilet-humor zombie shooter in multiplayer alpha. Jump into online 5v5 PvP, team up for round-based zombie co-op, or hold the line solo in first person, third person or top-down.

This is an early-access alpha. The game is in active development, updates land often and there are new events every week. Things may change as we go, and your feedback shapes what comes next.

What's new in 4.5.6: Lobbies + Gun Feel

  • New full-screen lobbies: map vote up top, both teams with every player's class and level
  • Back to your lobby after online matches
  • Change class mid-match in 5v5 and Infected
  • Holo-Poo rebuilt, every optic now mounted on the gun
  • 7 new attachments, guns level to 30
  • Snappier movement and smoother recoil
  • Lane signs and team banners on all 5v5 maps
  • Fixes for online rematches

Features

Multiplayer alpha: 5v5 PvP

  • Flush Wars: The Plungers vs The Brushes in online 5v5 matches
  • Team Dookiematch (first to 40) and Domination (hold 3 thrones, first to 150)
  • Infected: outlast the bots on a 3-minute clock
  • Rival 1v1: duel online, in a private match by code or over local Wi-Fi
  • Quick Play with a lobby map vote, playlists and join by code
  • Bots fill empty seats so matches start fast
  • Real hitboxes: headshots hit harder, with hitmarkers on every hit

Zombies: co-op survival

  • Zombies Online: co-op with up to 4 players (alpha)
  • Play solo with up to 3 AI squadmates, split screen on one device (player 2 on a controller) or over local Wi-Fi
  • Round-based survival: earn points, open doors, buy perks and upgrade your guns at the Pack-a-Turd
  • Six maps: Town, Sewer Subway, Dookie Desert, Bowel Movement, Stairway to Heaven and The Throne Room (endless)
  • Six bosses, from The Big Brown One to The Septic King and the Porcelain Emperor
  • Floods, sandstorms, acid tides and easter eggs that unlock wonder weapons
  • Switch to top-down view and see the whole horde coming

Guns, Gunsmith and levels

  • 27 weapons, including 6 wonder weapons
  • Gunsmith: 8 attachment slots, pick any 5 per gun, with saved builds (7 more attachments added in 4.5.6)
  • Level every gun up to 30 to unlock more attachments
  • Learnable recoil patterns, aim sway and real sights you look through
  • Create-a-Class: 5 classes, with lethals like the Sticky Stinker and Hot Sauce Bomb
  • Slide, dive, mantle and tactical sprint

Camo grind

  • Poop Mastery: grind camos on every gun, tier by tier
  • Earn Galaxy Duce on the original 19 guns to unlock The Divine Duce
  • Pack-a-Turd and prestige camos on top

Progress and rewards

  • 55 player levels plus prestige, with an unlock track for guns and gear
  • Daily login rewards, 3 daily contracts and 3 weekly contracts
  • 39 Google Play Games achievements and 24 leaderboards, including wins and kills for every playlist
  • Cloud save

Skins, Death FX, emotes and bundles

  • Stall Mart shop with featured bundles, event items and a daily rotation
  • Skins, Death FX, name plates, titles, calling cards and 30+ emotes on a 4-slot wheel
  • Your Death FX play when you flush an enemy in 5v5
  • Doo-bloons are earned by playing, and optional Doo-bloon packs are available as in-app purchases

Play your way

  • 18 languages
  • 90 and 120 FPS options for phones with fast screens, plus auto resolution to keep things smooth
  • Customizable touch controls, controller support, or keyboard and mouse with key rebinding
  • Color-blind modes and optional voice chat
  • Your hub is a 3D bunker you can stand up and walk around

Multiplayer and Zombies Online need an internet connection. Solo, split screen and local Wi-Fi zombies work offline.
