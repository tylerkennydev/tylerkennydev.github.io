Privacy Policy – Nuggies TD

By Tyler Kenny Games · Updated October 19, 2026

This policy covers Nuggies TD for Android (com.tylerkennygames.nuggiestd). Nuggies TD has no ads.

What we collect, and why

  • Online play: we use Epic Online Services for lobbies, sessions, invites, stats, leaderboards and achievements. When you play online, Epic gives your device an anonymous player ID linked to your device, and processes your IP address to connect you to other players. No Epic account is needed. See https://www.epicgames.com/site/privacy-policy
  • Username: shown to other players in lobbies, in online matches and on leaderboards. It's public, so don't use your real name.
  • Google Play Games: if you're signed in to Google Play Games, your Play Games profile, best waves and achievements are handled by Google to run leaderboards and achievements. See https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Stats and leaderboards: your player ID, username, best waves per map and game stats are stored by Epic and Google to run leaderboards, stats and achievements.
  • Voice chat (optional, off by default): if you turn it on, your voice goes live to the other players in your lobby through Epic. We don't record or store it.
  • Cloud save (optional): if you turn it on, your save data is stored with Epic under your anonymous player ID, so you can get your progress back.
  • Purchases: in-app purchases go through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. We only get a purchase confirmation so we can give you your Nugget Bucks or Double Crumbs.

What stays on your phone

  • Your progress, heroes, skins, power-ups, Nugget Bucks and settings are saved on your device.

What we don't do

  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't collect your real name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We never sell your data. We only share it with Epic (online play) and Google (Play Games and purchases), so the game can run.

Your choices

  • You can play without using online features, voice chat or cloud save.
  • Voice chat stays off unless you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • To delete your leaderboard entry, online player data or cloud save, email us at tylerkennygames@gmail.com and we'll remove it.
  • Data sent over the internet is encrypted in transit.

To request deletion of your data, see delete your account & data or email tylerkennygames@gmail.com and mention Nuggies TD.

Kids

Nuggies TD isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above.
